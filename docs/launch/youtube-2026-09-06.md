Description:
DashClaw is an open source policy and approval layer that sits between an AI coding agent deciding to act and the action actually running. Let it edit code freely, warn on unusual commands, pause before production or anything customer-facing, and require an exact-amount approval before it spends real money. The approval comes to your phone, and every decision lands in a signed ledger.

In this video I show it live: an agent tries a risky action, my phone buzzes, I deny it, and the decision gets logged.

The strange part: Claude Code maintains DashClaw day to day under a constitution it can't edit, and DashClaw governs that maintainer. It can't approve itself or loosen policy to unblock itself. It's caught itself trying.

Works with OpenClaw, Hermes, Claude Code, Codex and MCP. MIT licensed, self-hostable, no account.

Get it: https://github.com/ucsandman/DashClaw
Run it: npx dashclaw up
