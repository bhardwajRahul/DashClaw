## Tagline (<=60 chars, benefit, no jargon)

- Pick: **Approve or block what your AI agents do, from your phone** (56)
- Alt: Stop risky agent actions before they run (40)

## Description (~178 chars)

Let coding agents run for hours, but stop the risky actions before they run and approve them from your phone. Works with OpenClaw, Hermes, Claude Code, Codex and MCP. Every decision lands in a signed ledger.

## First comment (maker intro — post the second you launch)

I build with coding agents that run for hours, and I didn't want to hand them unconditional control of my machine, my repos, or my money to do it.

DashClaw sits between an agent deciding to act and the action running. A policy can let it edit code freely, warn on odd commands, pause before production or anything that reaches a customer, and require an exact-amount approval before it spends real money. The approval comes to my phone. Every decision lands in a signed ledger.

The honest origin: in June I got so annoyed at false interruptions I turned off all my policies for 18 days. So there's a calibration loop now, an interruption has to earn its cost.

The strange part: Claude Code maintains DashClaw day to day under a constitution it can't edit, and DashClaw governs that maintainer. It can't approve itself or loosen policy to unblock itself. It's caught itself trying.

MIT, self-hostable, no account: npx dashclaw up. I'd love criticism of the policy model and the self-governing maintainer idea.

## Video / interactive demo

- Video slot: a product demo under 2 minutes ("agent tries risky action -> phone buzzes -> I deny -> ledger logs it") beats a brand sizzle. A quick Loom of exactly that is worth more than an animation.
- Interactive demo slot (Arcade/Storylane, free for PH): a 5-click walkthrough of approving from the phone. For a dev tool this converts better than video. Optional but high value.

## Day-of playbook (2026 research)

- Hunters barely matter now; engagement does. Launch 12:01 AM PT, Tue-Thu.
- DM supporters individually, not a blast. Personal asks convert far better.
- Reply to every comment within ~15 minutes for the first few hours.
- Update the DashClaw homepage with a "Featured on Product Hunt" badge for the day.
- Caveat: cold PH launches rarely crack top 5 without a pre-built audience, and PH traffic converts worse than Indie Hackers. PH is reach and a badge, not your buyers. Worth doing, not worth over-investing.
