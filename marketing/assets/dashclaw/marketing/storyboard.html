CTA & positioning

Govern your agents at dashclaw.io

DashClaw governs the action itself. It holds the real command before it runs and binds the release to a recorded human decision, instead of showing you a log of what already happened.

Grounding

Audience

Developers who run coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw) unattended for hours and cannot watch every call

  • The agent reaches for a deploy, a table drop, a force push or a .env read while nobody is at the keyboard
  • Local permission prompts only work for the operator sitting at the terminal
  • Interrupt too often and the policies get switched off; the maintainer turned every policy off for 18 days after false interruptions
  • A log of what already ran is not control over what runs next
