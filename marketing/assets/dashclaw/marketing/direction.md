# DashClaw story film: direction (2026-09-03)

Three directions were written against docs/templates/DIRECTION.md; two were killed.

## Killed

**LEDGER OF LIGHT.** Abstract kinetic type on the dark ground, a line of light that
becomes a ledger row. Killed on question 3: any observability or audit tool could ship
it unchanged. A category template.

**GLASS CORRIDOR.** Photoreal office at night, a MetaHuman operator asleep at a desk,
the phone buzzing with the approval. Killed on question 4: a MetaHuman at 70% is
uncanny, and a photoreal interior on one RTX 3070 Ti is a 2am abandonment waiting to
happen. Also the weakest on question 1: the human is decoration, the product is the
hold.

## Survivor: NIGHT SHIFT

```
DIRECTION Night Shift
Thesis          At 3am an agent walks a dark data hall alone; the film is the moment
                it reaches for something that cannot be undone and the world stops
                until a person says yes.
Dials           energy: contemplative, long holds / density: one idea per frame /
                ground: a live 3D field (Unreal data hall) for the argument, rebuilt
                UI for the evidence / depth: deep 3D with real perspective / camera:
                continuous slow moves, cuts only at ground flips / type: mono
                (JetBrains Mono) that gets out of the way; the world carries the voice /
                texture: grain and halation, fog in the hall / colour: monochrome
                hall plus one signal, brand orange, with green only as the product's
                own finished state / product-literalness: metaphor in the hall,
                literal rebuilt UI in the inbox and ledger / sound: sound-design led,
                narration on top / voice: narrated, sparse, about 75 words in 40 s
Palette note    ground #0e1014 (brand.colors.bg) in both registers; hall surfaces
                are the surface tokens #15171c / #1d2026 with white slit lights;
                accent #f97316 is the agent itself (a point of light) and the held
                state; #22c55e appears only as the ledger's verified tick.
Type            JetBrains Mono 500 for every on-screen word except the end lockup's
                wordmark; type is timestamps, commands and ledger rows, never a
                headline until the last shot.
Signature move  THE HOLD. Whenever the agent reaches for a dangerous action the whole
                3D world freezes mid-motion (the light stops, the fog stops, the
                camera stops) while the interface decides. Three times: a two-second
                tease at the first rack (frame 330), the payoff before the inbox
                (frame 450), and as rhythm in the wide finale where dozens of agents
                freeze and release out of phase.
Sound           low room tone and rack fans, the agent as a soft rising tone, the
                hold as an abrupt full stop of every sound except the room, the
                release as fans coming back. Sound design leads; narration rides it.
Risk            At 70% the hall reads as a cheap game level. Guard: primitives only,
                no asset packs, fog and slit lights do the work, nothing photoreal is
                attempted, so nothing photoreal can fail.
Wrong for       a consumer app, anything playful, anything that wants a face.
```

**Ground map.** Opens on the argument ground (the hall). Flips to the evidence ground
(the inbox) at the payoff hold, back to the hall for the release, to the ledger, and
ends on the hall. Every flip is carried by the hold: the freeze is the transition.

**Dials different from the previous film (postflop, 2026-09-01):** ground, depth,
energy, type, product-literalness, sound, voice register. Seven of eleven. Arc differs
too: the climax is a stop, not a reveal; the ending is a wide, not a lockup swallowing
the frame (the lockup sits small over the wide).
