# DashClaw story film: storyboard for approval (2026-09-03)

Direction: NIGHT SHIFT (out/dashclaw/marketing/direction.md). Spec: film-spec.md.
40 s, 7 shots, two grounds. Board image: story-board.png (shots 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 are real
Unreal look-test frames; shots 4 and 6 are rebuilt DashClaw UI, built in Remotion after
approval, so the sixth tile is blank).

| # | shot | ground | what you see | narration |
|---|------|--------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | hall (0-5 s) | Unreal | Slow dolly down a dark aisle, white slit lights, fog, floor reflections. Mono timestamp 03:11:58 counting. | At three in the morning, your agent is still working. |
| 2 | agent (5-11 s) | Unreal | One orange point of light travels the aisle ahead of us; we follow. Caption `agent-7 · unattended`. | It reads. It writes. It deploys. Nobody is watching. |
| 3 | reach (11-15 s) | Unreal | The light stops at a rack showing `deploy --prod`; accelerates toward it. THE HOLD: the whole world freezes, an orange rule draws under the command, the clock stops at 03:12:14. | Until it reaches for something that cannot be undone. |
| 4 | intercept (15-22 s) | UI | Cut on the freeze to the rebuilt approvals inbox: a held-action card (HELD chip in orange, the command, risk 0.82, the bound hash, a waiting timer). Cursor clicks Allow; the chip flips to ALLOWED; a ledger row is born and slides away. | DashClaw holds it. Risk scored, bound to the exact command. Waiting for you, wherever you are. |
| 5 | release (22-27 s) | Unreal | Back on the frozen frame; the world unfreezes, the light passes into the rack, the rack pulses white once, the camera rises and pulls back until the aisle is one line among many. | One click. It runs. And the decision is on the record. |
| 6 | ledger (27-32 s) | UI | Rebuilt ledger: three rows waterfall in, a thin rule links the row to its decision id, one green verified tick. Caption `chained to the decision that allowed it`. | Every governed action, chained to the decision that allowed it. Not a log after the fact. |
| 7 | wide (32-40 s) | Unreal | High wide over six aisles, dozens of orange agents moving; every few frames one freezes and releases, out of phase. Small end lockup: mark, wordmark, `The approval layer for unattended agents.`, `dashclaw.io`. | Govern your agents at dashclaw dot io. |

Sound: room tone and rack fans lead; the agent is a soft rising tone; the hold is every
sound stopping except the room; the release is the fans returning; soft ticks on the
wide's freezes. Narration rides on top, 85 words.

Copy sources: end line = brief hook.altHeadlines[0]; inbox and ledger copy = brief
features[0] and [1]; url = brief cta. `risk 0.82`, `d-4f21`, `00:00:04` are
illustrative product UI values and will be labelled so in DISCLOSURE.md.

Known fix before the plate run: the fill light on the target rack in shot 3 reads warm
and wide (a wash); it becomes a small cool fill so the only orange is the agent and the
rule. Wide shot camera is 38 degrees down (not 25) so the whole hall is in frame.

Approve, or mark what changes, and the build starts: full plates (one Unreal launch,
~20 s warm), then the Remotion film, director loop, score, gates.
