# DashClaw — Instagram post kit

## Files
- Video: wrap-1x1.mp4
- Silent cut: wrap-1x1-silent.mp4 (muted-autoplay embeds)
- Thumbnail: NOT INCLUDED (segment kits omit the brand launch thumbnail)
- Caption: caption.txt (paste as the post copy)
- Alt text: alt.txt (one-sentence video description)


## Before you publish
1. Set the "AI info" labelling in the composer before publishing if this cut has synthetic voiceover or music (see DISCLOSURE.md at the kit root).
2. Destination link: https://dashclaw.io?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=dashclaw
3. After publishing, paste the live post URL into out/dashclaw/marketing/posts.json's `url` field for this platform (and set `published` to true).

## Notes
Upload as an Instagram feed post or Reel. Paste caption.txt as the caption and alt.txt into Advanced settings > Accessibility > Alt text.
